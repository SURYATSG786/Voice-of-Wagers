TRUST

Honest by design: we show the gaps

  • Wages say “needs checking.” Sourced, dated, never a legal ruling.
  • Estimates are tagged. Rent, food and savings carry an Estimated badge.
  • Records stay in scope. State-wide figures, never presented as city counts.
  • Nothing is invented. Missing data shows as unavailable.
  • You decide what to share. Pay notes stay on this app’s server. If AI is enabled, entered details may be sent to Gemini.
